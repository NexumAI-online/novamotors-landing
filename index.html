06 — FAQ

CLEAR BEFORE YOU START

The questions every client asks. No fine print.

ANOTHER QUESTION? WRITE US
When and who do I pay?

Only after inspection, report and your written approval. You pay the seller directly — we never touch the car money.

What if the inspection misses something?

If a prior defect slips through, we refund our fee and keep the car, reimbursing what you paid for it, landed in Spain.

What if a car fails inspection?

It's dropped at no extra cost and we move to the next unit on your list.

What will I pay in total?

Before buying you get the full landed cost with all taxes, worked through a real example.

How long does it take?

45–60 days from purchase; sourcing usually takes one or two weeks.

Can I import to resell?

Yes — we target models that sell well in Spain and run the margin before buying.

What about after delivery?

Mechanical warranty and maintenance in Spain through our partner workshops.

Can any car be imported?

Not all — some can't be homologated or need costly changes. We check before proposing a unit.
